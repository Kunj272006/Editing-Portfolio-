BEHIND THE SCENES

Decoding the AI Pipeline

From initial text prompts to final cinematic footage composition.

01

Concept & Text Prompting

We write rich visual descriptions containing detailed lighting specs (volumetric rays, high contrast), styling guides (Cyberpunk luxury, minimalist), camera lens emulations (35mm anamorphic), and motion prompts.

02

Generative AI Video Production

Using models like Kling, Veo, and Runway, we generate short High Definition clips. We iterate on seed codes to ensure consistency of character face geometry, clothing, and background environment properties.

03

Composite & Motion Grading

Clips are imported into Adobe After Effects where they are combined. We add volumetric glow filters, track 3D camera data for graphic overlays, perform sound mixing, and compile a cinematic final product.
